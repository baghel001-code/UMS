1Executive summary

The User Management System (UMS) controls who may use each company application and what they may do in it. The Asset Management System (AMS) keeps the register of company assets (equipment, software licences and stock), records who holds what, and runs requests, approvals, maintenance and audits.

1place where access is decided (UMS) for every application
7checks on every action: organisation, person, application, module, permission, location, policy
7asset-specific policies (hours, value limits, segregation of duties, MFA, own items…)
100%of changes and decisions are recorded with who, what, when

Key controls at a glance

No access without a role

Access comes only from roles given in UMS and from approved policies. Nobody, not even the AMS software, can bypass UMS.

Least privilege by location

Roles can be limited to stores, DCs or offices, and to a period (temporary cover ends by itself).

Segregation of duties

Nobody approves their own asset request. Approvers need MFA. Managers cannot dispose high-value assets.

Value-based limits

Assets over ₹1,50,000 are not issued to contract, intern or vendor staff. Refunds over ₹10,000 need a Store Manager.

Secure sessions

One device per person, automatic sign-out when idle, lockout after 5 wrong passwords, admins can end any session.

Fail safe

If the access service cannot be reached, nothing is shown or changed. A missing piece of information never grants access.

Changing a rule needs no software changeLimits, hours, roles and policies are data in UMS. An authorised administrator changes them in the UMS Admin Portal, and the change applies at once to every application. Every change is logged with its before and after values.

5Asset management business rules

5.1 Assets

IDRule
AM-01Every asset has a unique asset tag within the organisation, a name, a model (manufacturer and category), a status and a location.
AM-02Cost cannot be negative. Book value is calculated straight-line over the category's depreciation period; it reaches zero at the end of that period.
AM-03Status types decide what can happen: only Deployable assets can be issued or requested; Pending, Undeployable (e.g. Broken, Out for Repair) and Archived (Retired) cannot.
AM-04An Asset Manager sees and changes only assets of their locations, and cannot move an asset to a location outside them (both the current and the new record are checked).
AM-05Every employee can see the assets issued to them (My Assets), and nothing else unless their role allows it.

5.2 Issue (check-out) and return (check-in)

IDRule
AM-10An asset can be issued only to an active employee taken from the UMS directory, and only if it is not already issued and its status is Deployable.
AM-11Restrict Assets can be checked out only during store operating hours, 08:00–23:00.
AM-12Deny Assets costing more than ₹1,50,000 cannot be issued to contract, intern or vendor staff.
AM-13The expected return date, if given, cannot be in the past. Assets past their return date appear as overdue on the dashboard, in reports and in the daily digest.
AM-14A return (check-in) is possible only for an issued asset; the asset may be given a new status at return (e.g. Out for Repair).
AM-15The employee is e-mailed on issue and on return.

5.3 Requests and approvals

IDRule
AM-20Employees can request only assets marked Requestable, Deployable and not issued, and must give a reason.
AM-21A person can have only one pending request for the same asset.
AM-22Deny No self-approval: a request cannot be approved by the person who raised it.
AM-23Restrict Approving requests requires MFA to be enabled for the approver.
AM-24Approving also requires permission to issue that asset to that person, so AM-10 to AM-12 apply. Approval issues the asset immediately, and other pending requests for the same asset are rejected automatically ("Asset issued to …").
AM-25A rejection must state a reason. Requests can be decided or cancelled only while Pending.
AM-26Restrict A request can be seen or cancelled only by the requester, asset staff or auditors.
AM-27The asset desk is e-mailed for every new request (requester in copy); the requester is e-mailed the decision.

5.4 Software licences

IDRule
AM-30A licence has at least one seat. Seats cannot be reduced below the number in use.
AM-31A seat is given to a person and/or installed on an asset; a person can hold only one seat of the same licence.
AM-32No seat can be given when all seats are used or the licence has expired. Licences expiring within 90 days are flagged "renew soon"; those within 30 days appear in the daily digest.
AM-33Product keys are shown only to people allowed to edit the licence.
AM-34A licence can be deleted only when all seats are returned.

5.5 Accessories, consumables and components

IDRule
AM-40Accessories are lent to a person and returned; one of each item per person.
AM-41Consumables are issued and used up; the quantity issued cannot exceed stock; they cannot be returned.
AM-42Components are installed into an asset and return to stock when removed.
AM-43Quantities cannot be negative and cannot be reduced below the units currently out. An item's kind cannot change.
AM-44When available stock is at or below the re-order level the item is flagged and listed in the daily digest.
AM-45An item can be deleted only when all units are back in stock.

5.6 Maintenance and physical audits

IDRule
AM-50Maintenance needs a title. The cost cannot be negative, and the completion date cannot be before the start date. A record cannot be moved to another asset.
AM-51Location limits apply to maintenance as to the asset itself.
AM-52An audit confirms the asset was seen and sets the next audit date, which must be in the future. Audits due within 30 days are on the dashboard, in reports and in the digest.

5.7 Disposal (delete)

IDRule
AM-60An asset can be deleted only when it is checked in.
AM-61Deny Asset Managers cannot delete assets that cost more than ₹1,00,000; an Asset Administrator must.
AM-62Deny Contract, intern and vendor staff cannot delete anything (PO-09).

5.8 Master data

IDRule
AM-70Only Asset Administrators change master data (models, categories, manufacturers, suppliers, locations, departments, status labels); Asset Managers view it.
AM-71Names are required; category names are unique. Location codes are required, unique, and must match UMS user locations (e.g. S045, DC-NR1, HO-GGN) so that location limits work. A model needs a category and a manufacturer.
AM-72An entry in use by any record cannot be deleted.

7.2 Reports available to management

  • AMS Dashboard:
    • asset count and purchase value, utilisation (issued vs ready);
    • licence seats used, low stock, pending requests, open repairs;
    • breakdowns by category, location and status;
    • audits due, overdue returns, warranties ending within 90 days.
  • AMS Reports:
    • purchase value, book value and depreciation by category, location and status;
    • a detailed depreciation table, exportable to CSV.
  • Activity Log: every asset transaction, exportable.
  • UMS Dashboard:
    • users by status and department;
    • access decisions per application;
    • items needing attention;
    • recent changes.
  • Every figure respects the viewer's access. A location manager's report covers only their locations.

10How rules are changed

  1. Request. The process owner asks for a change (e.g. "raise the contract-staff limit to ₹2,00,000").
  2. Approve. The change is approved according to the company's change policy (Information Security and Audit for security-relevant rules).
  3. Test. The UMS Administrator checks the effect with the Access Simulator, which explains the decision step by step.
  4. Apply. The administrator edits the policy, role or setting in the UMS Admin Portal. It applies immediately to all applications; no software release is needed.
  5. Evidence. The Audit Trail records the change with old and new values, who made it and when.
Rules that need a software changeData rules that protect the integrity of the register (unique tags, no double issue, seat and stock limits, no deletion while in use) are built into the database and apply to every user. Changing them needs a development change and testing.
