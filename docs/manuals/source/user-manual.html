1Introduction

Vishal Mega Mart uses one central system, the User Management System (UMS), to decide who may use which application and what they may do in it. The Asset Management System (AMS) is one of those applications: it tracks every company asset, from laptops and POS terminals to software licences, toner and spare parts.

One account

You sign in to every application with your UMS account (your company e-mail and password).

Access decided by UMS

Your roles, the locations they cover and company policies (office hours, value limits) are kept in UMS. AMS asks UMS before every action.

Clear reasons

If something is not allowed, the screen tells you why, for example "role scoped to HO-GGN" or "only during operating hours".

3Signing in, signing out and staying secure

3.1 Sign in

  1. Open the AMS Web Portal (or the UMS Admin Portal).
  2. Enter your company e-mail and password, then click Sign in.
  3. You land on your start page: the Dashboard if you may see it, otherwise My Assets.

Your name and employee code appear at the top right. To leave, click Sign out. Signing out also ends your session in UMS.

3.2 One device at a time

When single-device login is switched on (it is, by default), you can be signed in to an application on one browser or device only. If you sign in somewhere else, the first browser is signed out at its next action and shows:

Did you not sign in elsewhere?Change your password at once and tell IT security: somebody may know your password.

3.3 Idle timeout

If you do not use the application for the tenant's idle time (30 minutes by default), you are signed out automatically. One minute before, a warning appears with a countdown. Click Stay signed in to continue, or Sign out. Activity in any tab of the same browser counts.

3.4 Lockout and passwords

  • After 5 wrong passwords in a row the account is locked for 30 minutes. The message says how long. A UMS administrator can also reset the password.
  • A new password needs at least 8 characters with an upper-case letter, a lower-case letter and a digit, and at least the tenant's minimum length (12 for Vishal Mega Mart).
  • Accounts that are disabled, or whose expiry date has passed (contract staff), cannot sign in.

4Employees: My Assets and asset requests

Every employee can see what is issued to them and ask for equipment. Sign in as Priya Verma or Imran Khan to try it.

4.1 My Assets

My Assets lists everything issued to you:

  • Assets: tag, model, serial number, issue date, the date to return it (if any) and location. Click an asset tag to open its details.
  • Software: the licence seats you hold.
  • Accessories & supplies: accessories on loan and recent consumables.

To return an item, take it to your IT / asset desk. They check it in and you receive an e-mail.

4.2 Request an asset

  1. Open Asset Requests and click Request an asset (also on My Assets).
  2. Choose an asset from the list. It shows assets that are offered for request, ready to deploy and not issued.
  3. Write why you need it and click Submit request.

The asset desk receives an e-mail. You get a copy, and another e-mail when the request is approved or rejected. When it is approved, the asset is issued to you and appears under My Assets.

  • You can have only one pending request for the same asset.
  • You can Cancel your own request while it is pending.
  • You see your own requests only.

6Asset Administrators: full asset management

Sign in as Rahul Agarwal to try everything in this chapter. The Dashboard shows:

  • key figures, with quick actions;
  • assets by category, location and status;
  • audits due in the next 30 days;
  • overdue check-ins;
  • warranties ending in the next 90 days;
  • recent activity.

6.1 Assets

An asset is a single tracked item with a unique tag (e.g. VMM-LT-1007): a laptop, desktop, POS terminal, printer, handheld or server. Each asset has:

  • a model, which gives its manufacturer and category;
  • a status such as Ready to Deploy, Out for Repair, Broken or Retired;
  • a location and, optionally, a department and supplier;
  • purchase date and cost, and a warranty period;
  • a Requestable flag: whether employees may request it.

Add an asset

  1. Click New asset.
  2. Fill in asset tag, name, model, status and location (required). Add serial, department, supplier, purchase date, cost, order number, warranty months and notes as needed.
  3. Tick Requestable if employees may request it. Click Save.

Check out (issue) and check in (return)

  1. Open the asset (click its row) or use the ⋮ menu in the list. Click Check out.
  2. Pick the employee from the UMS directory, optionally the expected return date and a note. Click Check out.
  3. The employee receives an e-mail. The asset shows "Checked out to …".
  4. When it comes back, click Check in, optionally choose its new status (e.g. Out for Repair) and add a note.
Only deployable, free assets can be checked outAn asset that is already issued, or whose status is not deployable (Broken, Retired…), is refused with a clear message.

Asset details

The detail page shows all data, the current value after depreciation, and a full history (created, edited, checked out and in, audited, licences installed). Tabs list maintenance, licences and components installed in it. The buttons at the top are those you are allowed to use.

Audit, labels, export, delete

  • Audit: confirms you saw the asset and sets the next audit date (must be in the future).
  • Print labels: printable tag labels (asset tag, name, location) for the assets in the list; filter by location first. Requires the Print permission.
  • Export CSV: the filtered list as a spreadsheet. Requires the Export permission and a managed device.
  • Delete: only for assets that are checked in. Managers cannot delete assets above ₹1,00,000.

Asset requests: approve or reject

Asset Requests shows pending requests by default. Click Approve to issue the asset to the requester at once, or Reject and give a reason. The requester is e-mailed either way. When a request is approved, other pending requests for the same asset are rejected automatically with the note "Asset issued to …".

6.2 Licences

A licence has a number of seats. A seat is given to a person, installed on an asset, or both. The bar shows seats used. A licence close to expiry shows renew soon, and an expired one shows expired; expired licences cannot be checked out. Product keys are shown only to users who may edit the licence.

  • Check out a seat (→ icon): choose a person and/or an asset.
  • Seat holders (people icon): see who holds seats; check a seat in from there.
  • The number of seats cannot be reduced below the seats in use.

6.3 Accessories, consumables and components

KindExamplesHow it works
AccessoriesMouse, headset, barcode scannerLent to a person and returned. One of each item per person.
ConsumablesToner, receipt rolls, labelsIssued and used up (cannot be returned). The quantity in stock goes down.

Each item has a quantity, a re-order level and a unit cost. When available stock reaches the re-order level the item shows re-order, and the daily digest e-mail lists it. Switch on Low stock only to see just those.

6.4 Maintenance

Record repairs, upgrades, calibration and warranty work against an asset: type, title, provider, start and completion dates, cost, warranty flag and notes. Open jobs only shows work not yet completed. You can also log maintenance from the asset's detail page.

6.5 Reports and activity log

Reports show:

  • purchase value, book value and depreciation, by category, location and status;
  • tabs for depreciation, warranties ending, audits due and overdue check-ins.

Export depreciation CSV downloads the full table. The Activity Log lists who did what and when, with filters and CSV export.

6.6 Setup lists

Under Setup the administrator maintains:

  • asset models, each with a manufacturer, a category and an end-of-life;
  • categories, with their depreciation period;
  • manufacturers and suppliers;
  • locations, whose codes must match UMS user locations, e.g. S045, DC-NR1, HO-GGN;
  • departments;
  • status labels, each of type Deployable, Pending, Undeployable or Archived.

An entry that is in use cannot be deleted; the "In use" column shows how many records use it.

7IT Support and Auditors: read-only access

  • IT Support Engineers see the AMS Dashboard and the asset list and details, to answer helpdesk questions such as "which laptop does this person have". They cannot change anything.
  • Internal Auditors can view every page and export CSV files (on a managed device), but cannot change data.

If you also hold another role (Sneha is both IT Support and Asset Manager), you get the combination of both.

8.2 Common tasks

Give someone Asset Management access for one location

  1. Users → open the person → Roles tab → Assign role.
  2. Choose Asset Manager. Under Scope (resource-based restriction) add location = HO-GGN (several locations: S045,S046).
  3. Optionally set Valid from / Valid to for temporary cover. Save the user.
  4. Check with Test access or the Access Simulator. The change applies at the person's next action; no restart or re-login is needed.

Onboard, change and offboard

  • New joiner: Users → New user (or CSV import). Fill in the profile (location and region matter for scopes and policies), give roles, set a password.
  • Contract staff: set Employment type = Contract and an Account expiry date; sign-in stops after that date.
  • Leaver: set Status to Inactive (or Locked). Then end their open sessions in Active Sessions.
  • Forgotten password: open the user and use Set password.

Change a rule

Edit the policy in ABAC Policies, for example raise the high-value limit or change the schedule. Every application, AMS included, applies the new rule immediately. Test it with the Access Simulator first.

8.4 Active sessions

Lists who is signed in to which application, the device, IP address, sign-in time and last activity. Switch off Active only to see ended sessions with the reason (signed out, idle, replaced by another device, ended by admin). Click the sign-out icon on a row to end a session, for example for a lost laptop. AMS signs that user out within a minute.
